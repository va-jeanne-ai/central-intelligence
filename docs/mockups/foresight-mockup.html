Why it isn't published: at n = 184 discovery calls the interval (58–91%) overlaps the baseline (30–46%) too little data to separate signal from noise. The engine keeps counting nightly and publishes automatically the week the intervals separate — or discards it if they converge.

Production rules: every number is a SQL count over the mirrors (lead_journey, closed_sales, email_campaigns, call insights) with Wilson score intervals — no ML, no heuristics, nothing a human can't reproduce with one query. Accepting a card starts an outcome window; results feed back into the card. Recommendations expire when their underlying rates converge.
